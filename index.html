    // professional experience

    Computational Biologist / PhD Researcher

    Gulbenkian Institute for Molecular Medicine (GIMM) / Instituto de Medicina Molecular, University of Lisbon — Feb 2022 – Present

    • Led a large-scale reanalysis of 16 independent public stress-granule transcriptomic datasets, correcting a previously unrecognised length-based technical bias and identifying mitochondrial RNA enrichment as a reproducible signal with a proposed role in immune signalling.
    • Co-led development of voyAGEr, a public web app processing RNA-seq data from 900+ individuals across 49 tissues (GTEx). Built statistical backend and interactive front-end.
    • Designed and built CHARM, an R Shiny analytics platform for similarity search, correlation analysis, and GSEA-based pathway enrichment of RNA-binding proteins, engineered for performance at scale.
    • Contributed to markeR, an open-source R package for gene set-level analysis, passing Bioconductor's formal peer review for public release.

    Research Intern, MSc Thesis

    Champalimaud Foundation — 2020 – 2021

    • Investigated cell competition using a colorectal cancer co-culture model; ran RNA-seq differential expression analysis comparing winner and loser cells.
    • Validated candidate genes by RT-qPCR and functional knockdowns, identifying three genes required for loser-cell elimination, and linked cell competition to cell migration and chemotherapy response for the first time in this cancer type.
